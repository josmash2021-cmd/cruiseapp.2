# Insurance Coverage Notice

**Version 1.0 — Effective Date: October 21, 2026**

This Insurance Coverage Notice (the "**Notice**") summarizes the
insurance-related provisions of the Cruiseinride Independent Contractor
Agreement (Drivers) (the "**Agreement**") between **Cruise in Ride, Inc.**
(the "**Company**") and the Driver. **This Notice is a summary and notice
only; the controlling terms are set out in the Independent Contractor
Agreement (Section 9), which prevails over this Notice in case of any
conflict.**

## 1. Driver's Personal Policy

The Driver shall maintain, at all times, a valid personal automobile
insurance policy meeting Florida minimums, including the
financial-responsibility and insurance requirements applicable to the Driver
under Fla. Stat. § 324.032(1) and Fla. Stat. § 324.221, and shall provide
proof of insurance upon request. **A personal auto policy may not cover
transportation network company activity**; the Driver should confirm TNC
coverage or an appropriate endorsement with the Driver's insurer.

The Driver shall keep the Driver's driver's license, vehicle registration,
insurance, and all other required documents current and valid at all times,
update the policy information in the app when it changes, and **immediately
notify the Company of any cancellation, lapse, expiration, or reduction** of
the Driver's required coverage.

## 2. Company-Maintained Coverage

Consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(7)–(8), the Company discloses that it
maintains automobile liability insurance for TNC activity at no less than the
following statutory minimums:

- **While the Driver is logged on but not engaged in a prearranged ride:** at
  least $50,000 for death and bodily injury per person, $100,000 for death
  and bodily injury per incident, and $25,000 for property damage, plus the
  personal injury protection required by Florida law.
- **During a prearranged ride (from acceptance through rider exit):** at
  least **$1,000,000** for death, bodily injury, and property damage, plus
  the personal injury protection required by Florida law.
- **Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage** as required by Fla. Stat.
  § 627.727 and the corresponding provisions of Fla. Stat. § 627.748(7).

The specific insurers, policy numbers, and any higher limits applicable to
the Driver are identified in the **Cruiseinride Insurance Disclosure**. The
Company will provide the Driver proof of coverage as required by Fla. Stat.
§ 627.748(7)(h), through the Cruiseinride Insurance Disclosure and upon
request at **support@cruiseinride.com**.

## 3. Accident Duties

In the event of an accident while the Driver is logged on or engaged in a
prearranged ride, the Driver shall: (a) comply with all duties imposed by
law; (b) report the accident to the Company without undue delay; (c)
cooperate in good faith with insurers, authorities, and the investigation and
defense of any claim; and (d) **disclose accurately whether the Driver was
logged on to the platform or engaged in a prearranged ride at the time of the
accident**, consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(8).
