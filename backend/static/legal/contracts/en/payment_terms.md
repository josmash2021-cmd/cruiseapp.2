# Driver Payment & Commission Terms

**Version 1.0 — Effective Date: October 21, 2026**

These Driver Payment & Commission Terms (the "**Payment Terms**") restate the
compensation provisions of the Cruiseinride Independent Contractor Agreement
(Drivers) (the "**Agreement**", Section 8) between **Cruise in Ride, Inc.**
(the "**Company**") and the Driver. In the event of a conflict, the Agreement
prevails.

## 1. Rider Fare and Upfront Display

1.1. **Rider fare.** For each trip, the passenger is charged a gross fare
calculated under the Company's pricing model as displayed in the app.

1.2. **Upfront fare display.** Before accepting a trip, the Driver is shown
the pickup location, the destination, and the trip fare for that trip, as
displayed in the app.

## 2. Service Fee and Driver Share

2.1. **Service fee and Driver share.** The Company retains a service fee of
**40%** of the gross trip fare (base fare, time and distance charges, and any
demand-based or surge pricing), and the Driver receives the remaining
**60%**. **No service fee applies to tips or to reimbursed tolls.**
Cancellation fees are defined and allocated separately under Section 4 of
these Payment Terms.

2.2. **Tips.** The Driver receives **100% of tips** paid by the passenger,
meaning the Company retains no portion of passenger tips, except for
adjustments required by law or resulting from payment errors, fraud,
chargebacks, or refunds.

2.3. **Tolls.** Tolls incurred during a prearranged ride are passed through
to the passenger and remitted to the Driver as displayed in the app.

## 3. Promotions, Incentives, and Adjustments

3.1. **Promotions and incentives.** The Company may offer promotions or
incentives. Participation is voluntary; the terms of each promotion will be
displayed in the app and will not penalize declining trip offers.

3.2. **Adjustments.** The Company may adjust payments to correct errors,
fraud, chargebacks, or refunds, and may make deductions only as permitted by
law, with itemized notice of any adjustment.

## 4. Cancellation Charges

4.1. Where a passenger cancellation fee applies under the Driver Terms of
Service, the fee is **$5.00**: the Driver receives **60% ($3.00)** and the
Company retains **40% ($2.00)**, as displayed in the app. No
payment-processing fee or other deduction is applied to the Driver's share.

## 5. Payment Cadence and Method

5.1. Driver earnings are paid **weekly — every Monday** — by ACH to the
Driver's linked bank account.

5.2. The Company also offers an optional **instant payout** for a fee of
**1.5%** of the payout amount (minimum fee **$0.50**), subject to a minimum
instant payout amount of **$50.00**.

## 6. Tax Documentation

6.1. The Driver shall provide a completed IRS Form W-9 before receiving
payments. The Company will issue information returns (including Form 1099-NEC
or 1099-K, as applicable) when required by law, and may apply backup
withholding or other legally required withholding.

## 7. Changes

Any change to the service fee will be communicated in advance. **Material
changes to these Payment Terms will be presented for re-acceptance before
taking effect as to the Driver.**
