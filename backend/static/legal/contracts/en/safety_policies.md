# Cruise in Ride Driver Safety Policies

**Version 1.0 — Effective Date: October 21, 2026**

These Driver Safety Policies (the "**Safety Policies**") apply to every
individual who drives on the Cruiseinride platform (the "**Driver**").
"**Cruiseinride**" means the transportation network company digital platform,
mobile application, and brand owned and operated by **Cruise in Ride, Inc.**
(the "**Company**"). These Safety Policies restate the safety obligations of
the Cruiseinride Independent Contractor Agreement (Drivers) (the
"**Contractor Agreement**") and the Cruiseinride Driver Terms of Service. In
the event of a conflict, the order of precedence of the Contractor Agreement
applies.

## 1. Zero Tolerance for Drugs and Alcohol

1.1. The Company maintains a **zero-tolerance policy** for the use of drugs
or alcohol while the Driver is logged on or providing rides, consistent with
Fla. Stat. § 627.748(10).

1.2. Upon receipt of a rider complaint alleging a violation of this policy,
the Driver **shall be suspended as soon as practicable for the duration of
the investigation, as required by law**.

## 2. Traffic and Safety Laws

2.1. The Driver shall comply with all applicable traffic, safety, and motor
vehicle laws.

## 3. Nondiscrimination, Service Animals, and Accessibility

3.1. **Nondiscrimination.** The Driver shall not discriminate against riders
or potential riders on the basis of any legally protected characteristic,
consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(14).

3.2. **Service animals and accessibility.** The Driver shall comply with laws
relating to the transportation of service animals and passengers with
disabilities, shall not refuse service on the basis of a service animal or
disability, and shall not impose additional charges for service animals or
for accommodations required by law, consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(14).

## 4. Ratings and Quality Thresholds

4.1. The Company operates a two-way rating system in which riders rate
Drivers after completed trips. Each Driver has a rating score that starts at
**5.0** and moves up or down by a fixed step for each rating received.

4.2. The following thresholds currently apply: (a) a score of **4.5 or
below** generates a warning to the Driver; (b) a score **below 4.3**
generates a notice that the Driver is at risk of deactivation; and (c) a
score of **3.5 or below** results in a **temporary 24-hour suspension** of
platform access, after which platform access is **automatically restored**
with the score set to **4.0**.

4.3. These thresholds apply only once the Driver has accumulated at least
**5** rated trips; no rating-based suspension is imposed before that minimum
is reached. A temporary suspension under this Section is not a deactivation;
the Company may convert it into a deactivation only after notice to the
Driver, a meaningful opportunity to improve, and an internal review under
Section 7 of these Safety Policies.

4.4. The Driver may request **manual review and early restoration** through
the internal review procedure in Section 7 — including review whenever the
Driver makes a plausible claim that the action resulted from discrimination
prohibited by law. These are rider-quality metrics, not acceptance-rate
metrics.

## 5. Cancellations and Fraud-Prevention Review

5.1. The Driver may cancel an accepted trip at any time before pickup through
the in-app cancellation function, **without Company pre-approval**, by
selecting a reason from the in-app list.

5.2. Each cancellation is logged (trip, driver, timestamp, location, reason,
and trip status) for **safety and fraud-prevention review**. **A cancellation
by itself does not reduce the Driver's dispatch priority or premium access,
and does not trigger suspension or deactivation**; only documented fraud,
safety issues, or abuse may have consequences, following review.

5.3. Once the rider is aboard, the Driver shall not cancel and shall instead
use the in-app safety / end-ride flow.

## 6. Background Checks and FCRA

6.1. The Driver authorizes the Company to obtain (a) an **initial**
background check report and driving history report before authorizing the
Driver, and (b) **subsequent reports at least once every three years**
thereafter, and as otherwise required by Florida law, consistent with Fla.
Stat. § 627.748(12).

6.2. Such reports are requested pursuant to the separate **"Background Check
Disclosure and Authorization"** document provided to the Driver. These Safety
Policies are not the sole disclosure for purposes of the Fair Credit
Reporting Act ("**FCRA**").

6.3. Any adverse decision based in whole or in part on a report will be
handled in accordance with the FCRA, including, where applicable, a
pre-adverse action notice, a copy of the report, the CFPB "Summary of Your
Rights Under the FCRA," and a final adverse action notice.

## 7. Suspension, Deactivation, and Internal Review

7.1. **Temporary suspension.** The Company may temporarily suspend platform
access: (a) during investigation of a reasonable safety, fraud, impairment,
or legal-compliance complaint; (b) upon expiration, lapse, or cancellation of
required documents or insurance, until corrected; or (c) as required by law.
The Company will state the reason for a suspension and, where the ground is
curable (such as expired documents), allow the Driver to cure and restore
access.

7.2. **Deactivation.** The Company may deactivate the Driver's account for
the grounds stated in the Driver Terms of Service, including legal or
regulatory requirements, safety, fraud, misrepresentation, or failure to
maintain required documents. Except where immediate action is required by law
or safety, the Company will provide notice of the reason.

7.3. **Internal review.** The Driver may request an internal review of a
suspension, deactivation, or rating-based action by contacting
**support@cruiseinride.com**. The Company will review the request and respond
within a reasonable period. **This review expressly includes actions based on
the rider-rating system, and the Company will conduct a review whenever the
Driver makes a plausible claim that any such action resulted from
discrimination prohibited by law.**

## 8. Account Security and Platform Integrity

8.1. The Driver shall use only the Driver's own approved account, profile,
driver's license, documents, and approved vehicle when providing services
through Cruiseinride, and shall not share the account, permit account access
by any other person, or substitute drivers or vehicles.

8.2. The Driver is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of the
Driver's login credentials and for all activity occurring under the Driver's
account, and shall promptly notify the Company of any unauthorized use.

8.3. The Driver shall complete identity verification steps when reasonably
required by the platform for security or legal compliance.

This document is provided for review and acceptance within the Cruise in
Ride Driver app.
