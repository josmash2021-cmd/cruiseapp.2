This Independent Contractor Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into by
and between **Royal Purple LLC**, a Florida limited liability company
("**Royal Purple**" or the "**Company**"), and the individual who accepts
this Agreement electronically through the Cruiseinride application (the
"**Driver**" and, together with the Company, the "**Parties**").

"**Cruiseinride**" means the transportation network company digital platform,
mobile application, and brand owned and operated by Royal Purple LLC. This
Agreement is separate from, and supplemental to, the Cruiseinride Driver
Terms of Service (the "**Terms of Service**").

## 1. Independent Contractor Relationship

1.1. **Written mutual acknowledgment.** The Company and the Driver expressly
agree, in writing, that the Driver is an **independent contractor** and not an
employee, agent, partner, franchisee, or joint venturer of the Company. This
written agreement is intended to satisfy Fla. Stat. § 627.748(9)(d).

1.2. **No employment relationship.** Nothing in this Agreement creates an
employment, agency, partnership, franchise, or joint venture relationship
between the Parties. The Driver has no authority to bind the Company or to
make representations on its behalf.

1.3. **No employment benefits; savings clause.** Subject to applicable
non-waivable law, the Driver is not entitled to wages, salary, overtime,
minimum wage, unemployment insurance, workers' compensation, paid leave,
retirement, health benefits, or any other employment benefit from the
Company. **Nothing in this Agreement limits, waives, or modifies any
federal, state, or local law that cannot lawfully be limited or waived, and
the Parties acknowledge that worker classification may depend on the actual
relationship between the Parties and not solely on the terms of this
Agreement.**

1.4. **Taxes.** The Driver is solely responsible for all federal, state, and
local taxes on the Driver's earnings, including self-employment tax, and for
any required estimated tax payments.

1.5. **Digital network only.** Royal Purple LLC provides a digital network
and **does not provide transportation services** and does not employ any
drivers, consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748. Transportation is provided
solely by independent contractor drivers.

## 2. No Schedule Control

2.1. The Company **does not prescribe, and shall not unilaterally prescribe,
specific hours, shifts, or schedules** during which the Driver must be logged
into the Cruiseinride platform, consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(9)(a).

2.2. The Driver decides **freely and exclusively** when, where, and for how
long to be available for trip requests, and may go offline at any time, for
any reason, without penalty. The Company imposes no minimum-hour quotas,
whether daily, weekly, or otherwise.

## 3. No Exclusivity

3.1. The Company **does not prohibit the Driver from using the digital
networks, applications, or platforms of any other transportation network
company or competitor**, at any time, including simultaneously with the
Driver's use of Cruiseinride, consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(9)(b).

3.2. Nothing in this Agreement requires the Driver to be available
exclusively on Cruiseinride.

## 4. No Restriction on Other Occupations

4.1. The Company **does not restrict the Driver from engaging in any other
occupation, profession, trade, or business**, whether or not related to
transportation, consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(9)(c).

4.2. No provision of this Agreement or of the Terms of Service constitutes a
covenant not to compete or an obligation of exclusive dedication.

## 5. Freedom to Accept or Decline Trips; Ratings

5.1. Every trip request is an **offer** the Driver may accept or decline at
the Driver's sole discretion. Declining, ignoring, or allowing an offer to
expire carries **no penalty**: it does not trigger suspension, deactivation,
reduced priority in dispatch, or loss of platform access.

5.2. Acceptance-rate metrics, where displayed, are **informational only** and
are not used to suspend, deactivate, penalize, or deprioritize the Driver.
The Company operates the platform consistent with this commitment.

5.3. The Company does not assign trips to the Driver without the Driver's
affirmative acceptance, including trips offered through the Company's
dispatch operations panel, which are transmitted only as offers.

5.4. **Driver cancellation before pickup.** The Driver may cancel an
accepted trip at any time before pickup through the in-app cancellation
function, **without Company pre-approval**, by selecting a reason from the
in-app list. Each cancellation is logged (trip, driver, timestamp, location,
reason, and trip status) for safety and fraud-prevention review. **A
cancellation by itself does not reduce the Driver's dispatch priority or
premium access, and does not trigger suspension or deactivation**; only
documented fraud, safety issues, or abuse may have consequences, following
review. Once the rider is aboard, the Driver shall not cancel and shall
instead use the in-app safety / end-ride flow.

5.5. **Ratings system.** The Company operates a two-way rating system in
which riders rate Drivers after completed trips. Each Driver has a rating
score that starts at **5.0** and moves up or down by a fixed step for each
rating received. The following thresholds currently apply: (a) a score of
**4.5 or below** generates a warning to the Driver; (b) a score **below
4.3** generates a notice that the Driver is at risk of deactivation; and
(c) a score of **3.5 or below** results in a **temporary 24-hour
suspension** of platform access, after which platform access is
**automatically restored** with the score set to **4.0**. These thresholds
apply only once the Driver has accumulated at least **5** rated trips; no
rating-based suspension is imposed before that minimum is reached. A
temporary suspension under this Section is not a deactivation; the Company
may convert it into a deactivation only after notice to the Driver, a
meaningful opportunity to improve, and an internal review under Section
14.6. In addition, the Driver may request **manual review and early
restoration** through the internal review procedure in Section 14.6 —
including review whenever the Driver makes a plausible claim that the
action resulted from discrimination prohibited by law. These are
rider-quality metrics, not acceptance-rate metrics (Section 5.2).

## 6. Platform Use and Account Security

6.1. The Driver shall use only the Driver's own approved account, profile,
driver's license, documents, and approved vehicle when providing services
through Cruiseinride, and shall not share the account, permit account access
by any other person, or substitute drivers or vehicles.

6.2. The Driver is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of the
Driver's login credentials and for all activity occurring under the Driver's
account, and shall promptly notify the Company of any unauthorized use.

6.3. The Driver shall complete identity verification steps when reasonably
required by the platform for security or legal compliance.

## 7. Driver's Vehicle, Expenses, and Trade Dress

7.1. The Driver uses a vehicle that the Driver owns, leases, or is otherwise
lawfully entitled to operate. **The Company does not own, rent, lease, lend,
assign, or act as bailee of any vehicle** used on the platform, consistent
with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(18)(a)3.

7.2. The Driver bears all operating expenses (fuel, maintenance, insurance,
depreciation, toll transponders, mobile data), except as expressly stated in
a written incentive communicated in the app. **Tolls** incurred during a
prearranged ride are handled as described in Section 8.

7.3. **Trade dress.** The Driver shall display Company trade dress only if
and to the extent (a) required by applicable law and (b) such trade dress is
provided or made available by the Company. When online, the Driver shall
display required trade dress only as mandated by Fla. Stat. § 627.748.

## 8. Compensation

8.1. **Rider fare.** For each trip, the passenger is charged a gross fare
calculated under the Company's pricing model as displayed in the app.

8.2. **Upfront fare display.** Before accepting a trip, the Driver is shown
the pickup location, the destination, and the trip fare for that trip, as
displayed in the app.

8.3. **Service fee and Driver share.** The Company retains a service fee of
**30%** of the gross trip fare (base fare, time and distance charges, and any
demand-based or surge pricing), and the Driver receives the remaining
**70%**. **No service fee applies to tips or to reimbursed tolls.**
Cancellation fees are defined and allocated separately under Section 8.6.
Any change to the service fee will be communicated in advance and, if
material, presented for re-acceptance under Section 22.

8.4. **Tips.** The Driver receives **100% of tips** paid by the passenger,
meaning the Company retains no portion of passenger tips, except for
adjustments required by law or resulting from payment errors, fraud,
chargebacks, or refunds.

8.5. **Tolls.** Tolls incurred during a prearranged ride are passed through
to the passenger and remitted to the Driver as displayed in the app.
*[Confirm practice.]*

8.6. **Cancellation charges.** Where a passenger cancellation fee applies
under the Terms of Service, the fee is **$5.00**: the Driver receives **70%
($3.50)** and the Company retains **30% ($1.50)**, as displayed in the app.
No payment-processing fee or other deduction is applied to the Driver's
share.

8.7. **Promotions and incentives.** The Company may offer promotions or
incentives. Participation is voluntary; the terms of each promotion will be
displayed in the app and will not penalize declining trip offers.

8.8. **Adjustments.** The Company may adjust payments to correct errors,
fraud, chargebacks, or refunds, and may make deductions only as permitted by
law, with itemized notice of any adjustment.

8.9. **Payment cadence and method.** Driver earnings are paid **weekly** by
ACH to the Driver's linked bank account. The Company also offers an optional
**instant payout** for a fee of **1.5%** of the payout amount (minimum fee
**$0.50**), subject to a minimum instant payout amount of **$50.00**.

8.10. **Tax documentation.** The Driver shall provide a completed IRS Form
W-9 before receiving payments. The Company will issue information returns
(including Form 1099-NEC or 1099-K, as applicable) when required by law, and
may apply backup withholding or other legally required withholding.

## 9. Insurance and Accident Duties

9.1. **Driver's personal policy.** The Driver shall maintain, at all times, a
valid personal automobile insurance policy meeting Florida minimums,
including the financial-responsibility and insurance requirements applicable
to the Driver under Fla. Stat. § 324.032(1) and Fla. Stat. § 324.221, and
shall provide proof of insurance upon request. **The Driver is advised that a
personal auto policy may not cover transportation network company activity**
and should confirm TNC coverage or an appropriate endorsement with the
Driver's insurer.

§ 627.748(7)–(8), the Company discloses that it maintains automobile

  (b) **During a prearranged ride (from acceptance through rider exit):** at
  least $1,000,000 for death, bodily injury, and property damage, plus the
  personal injury protection required by Florida law; and
  (c) **Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage** as required by Fla.
  Stat. § 627.727 and the corresponding provisions of Fla. Stat.
  § 627.748(7).

9.3. **Currency and updates.** The Driver shall keep the Driver's driver's
license, vehicle registration, insurance, and all other required documents
current and valid at all times, update the policy information in the app
when it changes, and **immediately notify the Company of any cancellation,
lapse, expiration, or reduction** of the Driver's required coverage.

9.4. **Accident duties.** In the event of an accident while the Driver is
logged on or engaged in a prearranged ride, the Driver shall: (a) comply with
all duties imposed by law; (b) report the accident to the Company without
undue delay; (c) cooperate in good faith with insurers, authorities, and the
investigation and defense of any claim; and (d) **disclose accurately whether
the Driver was logged on to the platform or engaged in a prearranged ride at
the time of the accident**, consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(8).

## 10. Background Checks and FCRA

10.1. The Driver authorizes the Company to obtain (a) an **initial**
background check report and driving history report before authorizing the
Driver, and (b) **subsequent reports at least once every three years**
thereafter, and as otherwise required by Florida law, consistent with Fla.
Stat. § 627.748(12).

10.2. Such reports are requested pursuant to the separate **"Background Check
Disclosure and Authorization"** document provided to the Driver. This
Agreement is not the sole disclosure for purposes of the Fair Credit
Reporting Act ("**FCRA**").

10.3. Any adverse decision based in whole or in part on a report will be
handled in accordance with the FCRA, including, where applicable, a
pre-adverse action notice, a copy of the report, the CFPB "Summary of Your
Rights Under the FCRA," and a final adverse action notice.

## 11. Safety, Zero Tolerance, and Nondiscrimination

11.1. **Zero tolerance.** The Company maintains a zero-tolerance policy for
the use of drugs or alcohol while the Driver is logged on or providing rides,
consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(10). Upon receipt of a rider complaint
alleging a violation of this policy, the Driver **shall be suspended as soon
as practicable for the duration of the investigation, as required by law**.

11.2. **Traffic and safety laws.** The Driver shall comply with all
applicable traffic, safety, and motor vehicle laws.

11.3. **Nondiscrimination.** The Driver shall not discriminate against riders
or potential riders on the basis of any legally protected characteristic,
consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(14).

11.4. **Service animals and accessibility.** The Driver shall comply with
laws relating to the transportation of service animals and passengers with
disabilities, shall not refuse service on the basis of a service animal or
disability, and shall not impose additional charges for service animals or
for accommodations required by law, consistent with Fla. Stat. § 627.748(14).

## 12. Street Hails and Off-Platform Trips

12.1. The Driver shall not: (a) solicit or accept street hails; or (b) divert
off-platform any trip, rider, or payment **solicited, arranged, or obtained
through Cruiseinride**.

12.2. **Scope.** This Section restricts only the diversion of
platform-originated business. It does **not** prevent the Driver from
engaging in any other occupation, business, or lawful transportation service
independent of Cruiseinride, and shall be read consistently with Sections 3
and 4.

## 13. Privacy and Data

13.1. The Company's Privacy Policy governs the collection and use of personal
information. The Company may use Driver data for platform operation, safety,
legal compliance, payments, insurance, and verification purposes.

13.2. The Driver may access and use rider personal information **solely to
complete the trip and as otherwise permitted by law**. The Driver shall not
retain, sell, disclose, or use rider personal information for any purpose
**unrelated to completing the trip**, and shall delete rider contact details
when no longer needed for the trip.

## 14. Term, Termination, Suspension, and Deactivation

14.1. **Term.** This Agreement takes effect upon electronic acceptance and
continues until terminated under this Section.

14.2. **Termination by the Driver.** The Driver may terminate this Agreement
at any time by discontinuing use of the platform and providing notice through
the app or to **support@cruiseapp.com**.

14.3. **Termination by the Company.** The Company may terminate this
Agreement by notice to the Driver. Termination of this Agreement ends the
contractual relationship prospectively and does not itself allege cause.
Termination is subject to applicable non-waivable law and does not affect the
Company's obligation to pay amounts lawfully owed for services already
completed.

14.4. **Temporary suspension.** The Company may temporarily suspend platform
access: (a) during investigation of a reasonable safety, fraud, impairment,
or legal-compliance complaint; (b) upon expiration, lapse, or cancellation of
required documents or insurance, until corrected; or (c) as required by law.
The Company will state the reason for a suspension and, where the ground is
curable (such as expired documents), allow the Driver to cure and restore
access.

14.5. **Deactivation.** The Company may deactivate the Driver's account for
the grounds stated in the Terms of Service, including legal or regulatory
requirements, safety, fraud, misrepresentation, or failure to maintain
required documents. Except where immediate action is required by law or
safety, the Company will provide notice of the reason.

14.6. **Internal review.** The Driver may request an internal review of a
suspension, deactivation, or rating-based action by contacting
**support@cruiseapp.com**. The Company will review the request and respond
within a reasonable period. **This review expressly includes actions based on
the rider-rating system, and the Company will conduct a review whenever the
Driver makes a plausible claim that any such action resulted from
discrimination prohibited by law.**

14.7. **Effect of termination.** Upon termination or deactivation: (a) the
Company will pay all amounts lawfully owed for completed services, subject to
lawful adjustments; (b) the Driver shall cease displaying Company trade dress
and, at the Company's option, return or destroy Company-provided materials;
(c) the Driver's platform access ends; and (d) provisions that by their
nature should survive (including Sections 1.4, 8.10, 9, 13, 16 through 20,
and 23) survive.

## 15. Intellectual Property

15.1. The Company owns all rights in Cruiseinride, including its marks,
software, and content. The Company grants the Driver a limited, revocable,
non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the app and Company-provided
trade dress solely to provide services under this Agreement. The Driver
retains all rights in the Driver's own materials and grants the Company a
limited license to use the Driver's name, photo, and profile information to
operate the platform.

## 16. Indemnification

16.1. To the extent permitted by law, the Driver shall indemnify and hold
harmless the Company from third-party claims, damages, and expenses arising
out of the Driver's (a) violation of law, (b) negligent or willful misconduct
in providing services, or (c) material breach of this Agreement. This Section
does not require indemnification for claims arising from the Company's own
negligence, willful misconduct, or breach, or to the extent coverage is
provided under an insurance policy described in Section 9.

## 17. Limitation of Liability

17.1. To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Company shall not be liable
for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising
out of this Agreement. Nothing in this Section limits liability that cannot
lawfully be limited, or the insurance obligations described in Section 9.

## 18. Dispute Resolution

18.1. The Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute
arising out of this Agreement informally by notice under Section 20.

18.2. **Binding individual arbitration.** Except as provided in Sections
18.5 through 18.8, any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or
relating to this Agreement that is not resolved informally shall be resolved
by **final and binding individual arbitration** administered by the American
Arbitration Association ("**AAA**") under its applicable Consumer or
Commercial Arbitration Rules. The arbitration shall be seated in **[COUNTY]
County, Florida**, shall be conducted in English before a single arbitrator,
and the arbitrator shall apply the laws of the State of Florida. Judgment on
the award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

18.3. **CLASS ACTION WAIVER.** THE COMPANY AND THE DRIVER AGREE THAT EACH MAY
BRING CLAIMS AGAINST THE OTHER **ONLY IN AN INDIVIDUAL CAPACITY** AND NOT AS
A PLAINTIFF OR CLASS MEMBER IN ANY PURPORTED CLASS, COLLECTIVE, CONSOLIDATED,
OR REPRESENTATIVE ACTION, AND NOT IN ANY PRIVATE ATTORNEY GENERAL ACTION. THE
ARBITRATOR MAY NOT CONSOLIDATE THE CLAIMS OF MORE THAN ONE PERSON.

18.4. **JURY TRIAL WAIVER.** TO THE EXTENT ANY CLAIM PROCEEDS IN COURT RATHER
THAN ARBITRATION, THE COMPANY AND THE DRIVER EACH **WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A
TRIAL BY JURY**.

18.5. **Small-claims carve-out.** Either Party may bring an individual claim
in the small-claims court of **[COUNTY] County, Florida**, so long as the
claim qualifies and remains in that court.

18.6. **30-day opt-out.** The Driver may opt out of this arbitration
agreement by emailing **support@cruiseapp.com** within **30 days** of first
accepting this Agreement, including the Driver's name, account email, and a
clear statement that the Driver opts out of arbitration. If the Driver opts
out, disputes shall be litigated in the state or federal courts located in
**[COUNTY] County, Florida**, still on an individual basis only — the class
action waiver in Section 18.3 survives opt-out to the fullest extent
permitted by law.

18.7. **Intellectual property.** Either Party may seek injunctive or other
equitable relief in a court of competent jurisdiction for actual or
threatened infringement or misappropriation of intellectual property rights.

18.8. **FCRA exclusion.** This arbitration agreement does not apply to the
background-check pre-adverse action and dispute process under the Fair Credit
Reporting Act ("**FCRA**") described in Section 10 and in the Background
Check Disclosure and Authorization; that administrative process remains
unchanged.
