# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: December 2024**

## Introduction

Cruise ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App") and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Registration**: Name, email address, phone number, profile photo, date of birth
- **Driver Information**: Driver's license, vehicle registration, insurance information, banking details (for payouts)
- **Identity Verification**: Selfie, government-issued ID, SSN (encrypted)
- **Payment Information**: Credit card details, payment method information (processed through Stripe)
- **Location Data**: Real-time GPS coordinates, pickup/dropoff locations
- **Communication**: Messages, support tickets, chat history

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system, device ID, IMEI
- **Usage Data**: App features used, trip history, payment history, ratings and reviews
- **Location Data**: Continuous GPS tracking during trips for navigation and safety
- **Log Data**: API requests, crash reports, analytics events
- **Cookies & Tracking**: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics for app performance monitoring

### 1.3 Third-Party Sources

- **Background Check Data**: Checkr integration provides background check results
- **Payment Processors**: Stripe provides payment and transaction information
- **Social Media**: Google and Apple OAuth provide account information when you sign in

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for:

- **Service Provision**: Creating and maintaining your account, processing rides, payments, and earnings
- **Safety & Security**: Background checks, fraud prevention, identity verification
- **Communication**: Support inquiries, trip notifications, service updates
- **Analytics & Improvement**: Understanding usage patterns, improving app features
- **Legal Compliance**: Tax reporting, regulatory requirements, legal obligations
- **Marketing**: Promotional emails (which you can opt out of)
- **Push Notifications**: Trip updates, driver/rider notifications
- **Location Services**: Real-time tracking during trips, ETA calculations

## 3. Sharing Your Information

### 3.1 We Share Information With:

- **Drivers/Riders**: Names, ratings, photos, phone numbers (limited to active trip participants)
- **Payment Processors**: Stripe receives payment card information
- **Background Check Provider**: Checkr receives personal information for verification
- **SMS Provider**: Twilio receives phone numbers for OTP and notifications
- **Email Services**: EmailJS, Mailgun for transactional emails
- **Analytics**: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics for crash reporting
- **Support**: Anthropic Claude processes support chat messages

### 3.2 We Do NOT:

- Sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes
- Share your SSN with anyone except tax/legal authorities
- Disclose your location except to trip participants during active rides
- Share payment card details with anyone except Stripe

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption**: SSN encrypted with AES-256, passwords hashed with bcrypt-12, HTTPS/TLS 1.3 for all communications
- **Authentication**: JWT tokens with expiration, multi-factor OTP verification
- **Rate Limiting**: Protection against brute force attacks
- **IP Blacklisting**: Automatic ban after suspicious activity
- **Database Security**: Parameterized queries to prevent SQL injection
- **Access Control**: Role-based access, admin audit logging

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

### 5.1 Access & Portability
You can request a copy of your personal data in a portable format.

### 5.2 Correction
You can update your profile information directly in the app.

### 5.3 Deletion
You can request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal/regulatory requirements).

### 5.4 Opt-Out
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails via link in email footer
- Disable push notifications in app settings
- Turn off location sharing (though this limits trip functionality)

### 5.5 Do Not Track
We do honor the "Do Not Track" signal for analytics (Crashlytics will be disabled).

## 6. Retention of Data

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained for duration of account + 2 years
- **Deleted Accounts**: Personal data deleted within 30 days (except where legally required)
- **Trip History**: Retained for 7 years for tax/legal purposes
- **Support Chats**: Retained for 1 year
- **Location Data**: Real-time data not stored; trip routes stored with trip records

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 18 years. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the data immediately.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data privacy regulations that differ from your home country.

## 9. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their privacy policies independently.
